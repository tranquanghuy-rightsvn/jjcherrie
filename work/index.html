2026/ Brand Identity

VME Brand Identity

A contemporary rebrand for Vietnam’s premier cultural institution. This project modernizes the museum’s visual identity by fusing traditional ethnic textile motifs with a modern minimalist grid. The result is a cohesive and accessible brand system that celebrates cultural diversity while appealing to the modern museum visitor, ensuring that heritage remains a living, breathing conversation.

2025/ UI Design

DreamWeaver Mobile App

A holistic mobile app concept that merges sleep cycle tracking with AI-driven dream interpretation. Designed with a calming, nocturnal UI, DreamWeaver empowers users to decode subconscious patterns, improve sleep hygiene, and turn cryptic nightly visions into actionable daily insights - all wrapped in an immersive, ethereal user experience.

2025/ Publication Design

Alice In Wonderland

A joyful, anime-infused publication design for Lewis Carroll's classic, brought to life entirely through AI-generated imagery. Featuring vibrant candy-colored palettes, expressive character art, and a consistently cheerful atmosphere, this project reimagines Wonderland as a bright, pop-inspired visual feast. The layout design harmonizes these dynamic AI illustrations with elegant typography, creating a collectors' edition that feels modern, playful, and irresistibly optimistic.

2024/ Publication Design

Grids And Pages Layouts

A complete redesign and localization of Amy Graver & Ben Jura's typography guide. This project translates the full English content into Vietnamese while transforming the book's neutral aesthetic into a dynamic, colorful layout system. By layering bright geometric shapes and a vibrant palette over a structured grid, the redesign turns technical theory into an inspiring visual experience, making essential design knowledge more accessible and engaging for a Vietnamese creative audience.

2024/ Brand Identity

Ho Chi Minh Heritage Museum

A complete brand identity redesign for the Ho Chi Minh Heritage Museum, shifting the institution's image from traditional to youth-friendly. The new visual language employs basic geometric shapes and a bright, energetic color palette to create an approachable, contemporary identity. The name change reflects a renewed mission to make Vietnamese heritage feel accessible, vibrant, and relevant to a new generation of visitors.

2024/ UI Design

DeliFresh Mobile App

DeliFresh is a mobile app that combines fresh grocery delivery with personalized meal suggestions. Designed with a clean, intuitive interface and adorned with cute hand-drawn illustrations, the app transforms everyday shopping into a warm, enjoyable experience. The visual identity balances functionality with charm, making healthy eating feel accessible, fun, and effortlessly simple.
